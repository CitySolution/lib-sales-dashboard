Other TikTok items are reimbursements and chargebacks on each box's orders, and its share of the FBT warehouse fee (each month's fee shared across the boxes sold that month). Shop-level items are the rest: anything whose order isn't in the uploaded files, and warehouse fees for a month with no sales. Bill payments (topping up a negative balance) aren't income or costs and are left out.
