Add new TikTok figures

  1. In TikTok Seller Center go to Finance → Statements.
  2. Click Export, choose any date range that covers the new statements (overlapping earlier uploads is fine), and download the .xlsx file.
  3. Choose the file(s) below, check the summary and press Add to dashboard. Statements already in the dashboard are skipped automatically.
